Who He Is

About Gideon

Gideon Rono is a dedicated Computer Studies and ICT teacher with a passion for empowering the next generation of digital citizens in Kenya. With a deep commitment to learner-centred education and technology integration, he designs lessons that connect theoretical knowledge with real-world problem-solving.

As a CBC Facilitator, Gideon works closely with fellow educators to implement competency-based learning strategies, develop learner portfolios, and align teaching practices with Kenya's evolving curriculum framework. He believes that a great teacher never stops learning — and he lives this philosophy every day.

Beyond the classroom, Gideon is passionate about environmental conservation, enjoys football, volleyball, music, and dancing — a reminder that the most effective educators bring their whole selves to their work.

Teaching Approach

Teaching Philosophy — Learner-Centred Excellence

Gideon's approach to teaching is grounded in a deep belief that every student has the capacity to thrive when given the right support, tools, and challenge. He designs lessons around real-world ICT applications that students encounter daily — from digital communication to spreadsheet analysis — ensuring that knowledge is always actionable.

He prioritises mentoring over lecturing. In his classroom, questions are celebrated, mistakes are learning opportunities, and problem-solving is a collaborative endeavour. His goal is not merely to teach students what to answer, but to build their capacity to ask better questions.

Curriculum Facilitation

CBC Facilitation — Shaping Kenya's Curriculum

As a CBC Facilitator, Gideon plays a pivotal role in helping teachers navigate Kenya's competency-based curriculum. He conducts in-school workshops, models best practice, and helps educators develop assessment tools that measure learner competencies — not just content recall.

He supports the development of learner portfolios, CBC-aligned rubrics, and project-based tasks that encourage critical thinking, creativity, and collaboration. His facilitation work has touched multiple teachers across his region, strengthening the foundation of CBC implementation at the school level.

Technology & Digital Literacy

ICT & Technology — Bridging the Digital Divide

Gideon is a committed technology-in-education advocate. He integrates ICT tools into his lessons to enhance engagement, track learner progress, and connect students with global learning resources. From digital portfolios to online collaboration platforms, he helps students build the skills Kenya's digital economy demands.

He teaches digital literacy, basic programming concepts, web fundamentals, and productivity applications — ensuring students leave his classroom not just as passive technology consumers, but as confident, creative digital citizens ready for tomorrow's challenges.
